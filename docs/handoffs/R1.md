## Design
- **`BodyConfig`** (`options.Body`, new `Simulation/SimulationWorld.Sleep.cs`):
  - **off by default** (`BodyConfig.Default`), so every earlier scenario and test keeps its behaviour
  - **the game turns it on** (`SimulationBridge`: `Body = BodyConfig.Realistic`); the benchmark has `--body`
  - all R1 behaviour is behind `Enabled`
- **Species bodies:**
  - `SpeciesDef.HungerPercent` / `ThirstPercent`: rabbit 150/130 (small, eats often), wolf 70/90 (big meals), dragon 30/40 (eats rarely), others 100
  - the percentages are carried inside the vitals anchor (`VitalsAnchor.HungerPct/ThirstPct/RegenPct`), and every assignment to `EntityState.Vitals` stamps the entity's current rates. That keeps every existing vitals read and death booking correct without touching the callers (several are in R2's files). The rates follow from hashed state (species table, rest state), so they are not saved separately and are re-stamped after load.
- **Sleep** (`ActionKind.Sleep = 12`, `SpeciesDef.Sleep`): diurnal creatures sleep 20:00–05:00, nocturnal ones (wolves) 09:00–16:00.
  - **When:** fed and safe; candidate score 280, above work and reading and below urgent eating or drinking.
  - **Where:** townsfolk walk to their town centre first (a path like Read; they sleep where they are if the path fails); animals sleep where they stand.
  - **Guards keep watch** and don't sleep.
  - **Sleep effects:** fatigue recovers 2× faster than resting; healing is 400% asleep and 250% resting.
  - **Waking:**
    - sleepers notice threats only within 2 tiles
    - any `Attacked` within 6 tiles wakes them (`EventKinds.WokeUp = 225`)
    - otherwise they wake when the window ends
  - Rest-state changes re-anchor both fatigue and vitals, so the elapsed-time anchors stay exact.
- **Presentation:** sleepers use the lying pose (without the dead tint).

## Evidence (2026-11-25)
- `dotnet test Tests/Simulation.Tests -c Release -m:1` → **117/117** groups (golden re-pinned for schema 16). New R1 groups:
  - **Species rates:** exact hunger and thirst after 60 ticks for rabbit, deer, wolf, human and dragon, with realism on (species %) and off (all 100%).
  - **Sleep cycle:**
    - at 03:00 every non-guard villager is asleep at the town centre, the guard is awake, and the wolves are awake
    - at noon nobody sleeps and the wolves do
    - everyone sleeps again the next night
  - **Woken by a fight:** a wolf 4 tiles away goes unnoticed. Once the guard fights it, sleepers wake (`WokeUp`) only at ticks with a fight within 6 tiles, and nobody wakes before the first blow.
  - **Healing:** in 100 ticks, an awake villager gains exactly 100 × regen and a sleeping one exactly 400% of that.
  - **Save while asleep:** a full day after load is identical.
- Benchmark, 1,000 entities / 3,000 ticks, realism off: p50 1.29 ms, p99 4.70 ms. With `--body`: p50 1.15 ms, p99 3.36 ms. The off p99 is above the earlier 2.6 ms; this is run-to-run noise or from changes merged since (not R1, which is off in that run).
- Godot: T007, T008, GodTools, Settlement, Effects and Mood acceptances pass. The Effects acceptance now records the impact on the hit's frame, because with sleep the night-time dragon fight lasts longer.
- `docs/handoffs/R1-night.png`: Ashford at 01:39 with everyone asleep at the lit store and a deer asleep in the field.
