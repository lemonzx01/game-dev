## Design
- **Skills:** Farming, Foraging, Woodcutting, Combat, Trading, Scholarship. XP per member (`long[6]`); level = min(10, √(xp/100)). XP comes only from what actually happened:
  - harvested units × 10 (Farming)
  - gathered units × 10 (Foraging for food, Woodcutting for wood)
  - landed hits × 20 (Combat)
  - units sold × 5 (Trading)
  - pages read × 1 (Scholarship)
- **Effects:**
  - Harvest and gather loads are capacity + level/2.
  - Each Combat level adds 10% damage.
  - Each Scholarship level adds 10% XP to the other skills.
  - `SkillLevelUp` (220) is emitted on each new level.
- **Reading (`ActionKind.Read`):**
  - Each town keeps one book (`Settlement.Books`, 300 pages). A fed, safe, adult member whose town has no work for them walks to the town centre and reads in sessions of up to 100 ticks (1 page per tick).
  - When a session ends for any reason (finished, interrupted by danger, defence, hunger, death or idle switch), `FinishReading` credits exactly `min(ticks elapsed, pages left)`, stores the per-book progress and awards Scholarship XP for those new pages only. It emits `ReadProgress` (221; Reason 1 = book finished).
  - Resumed sessions continue from the stored page. A finished book is never read again, so XP can never be awarded twice.
- **New state:** `SkillConfig` in options, member skills, reading list and open session, settlement books. StateHash and save schema 12. The Godot inspector shows skills and books.

Later sessions resume and finish the book. Total pages = 300 = Scholarship XP. No read starts after the book is finished, and Scholarship levelled up.
  - **Work XP:** with no scholar bonus, every member's Farming, Foraging and Woodcutting XP equals 10 × the units in its Harvested and Gathered events.
  - **Skilled farmers:** at level 10, farmers harvest more than carry capacity per trip; novices never do.
  - **Save mid-reading:** the loaded world continues identically.
