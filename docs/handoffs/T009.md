## Design
- **Species data:** `Data/species.json` + `SpeciesCatalog` (loader via System.Text.Json at construction time, never in Step; validation of keys, names, diets, speeds, references, hunting rules, domestic leash). Roster: Deer (wild herbivore), Sheep (farm animal, domestic, leash 6), Rabbit (fast wild herbivore), Wolf (carnivore, hunts all three). `SimulationOptions.Species` defaults to empty (every species is a plain herbivore), so older scenarios are unchanged; the headless runner and new scenarios use `SpeciesCatalog.Default`. Per-species move speed (`MoveTicksPerTile`).
- **Meat is a real resource:** any death (vitals or predation) leaves a carcass holding the body's `MeatYield` on its tile (one carcass per tile; bodies on the same tile add up). Meat leaves only by being eaten (`EntityAte`, Reason = Meat, Amount = units) or by decay after `CarcassDecayTicks` (`CarcassDecayed`, Amount = meat removed: explicit sink). Nothing creates meat from nothing.
- **Predation:** carnivores' Eat goal is carcass meat; if none is reachable (path failure → cooldown) they Hunt the nearest perceived prey: greedy chase, attack when adjacent every `AttackIntervalTicks`, `Attacked` events, health re-anchored; at 0 health the prey dies via the same `Die` path with `DeathCause.Predation` and the killer recorded (event Actor, durable record, `ReadHistory` Actor). Hunts give up after `HuntGiveUpTicks` with a cooldown. The killer's food cooldown is cleared so it eats its kill.
- **Diets:** herbivores cannot eat meat and carnivores cannot eat plants (`WrongDiet`); omnivores try plants then meat. Fear comes from species data (plus the older `MindConfig.FearOf`).
- **Farm animals:** domestic species only wander to tiles within `LeashRadius` of home (the spawn tile), or toward home if outside it.
- StateHash schema 6 (species table, ecology config, carcasses, killer, hunt state).

## Open items
- Vitals rates are global, not per species (wolves and rabbits get hungry at the same rate). Add per-species vitals when balancing (T014).
- No reproduction yet, so populations only decline (T011 birth/ageing).
- Hunting is greedy chasing with no pack behaviour; prey food choice is not risk-aware.
- Carcasses are not in snapshots yet; the Godot view (T007/T008) cannot show them until a read model is added.
