## What changed
- **Dragon sprite.** `Game/Assets/Art/dragon_48.png` (CC0) is generated by `tools/art/make_dragon.py`.
  - The script reuses the outline and shading helpers of `make_characters.py`.
  - It draws an emerald dragon with bat wings, horns and a spade tail.
  - Its 13 columns match the character sheet: wing flaps while walking, head down to eat, rearing, and a lunge with fire in its jaws.
  - `MapRenderer` draws species 7 from this 48 px sheet. It is mirrored and depth-sorted like everyone else.
- **Effects** (`Game/Presentation/EffectsLayer.cs`, in a camera-following `FxLayer` CanvasLayer so the night tint does not dim the glow). The pixelfx sprite sheets are in `Game/Assets/Effects` (MIT, licence file included; provenance in `Assets/CREDITS.md`). Effects are driven only by real drained events:
  - `SpellCast` Firebolt: a fireball flies from the caster to the target, then lands as an orange impact. The `Attacked` event from the same bolt does not play a second hit.
  - `SpellCast` Heal: a small green ring and rising light on the healed person.
  - `Attacked`: an impact effect on the target.
  - `AnimalTamed`: a sparkle burst.
  - Effects are capped at 96 at once, so a fast-forwarded battle drops extras.
- **Inspector:** mana / max mana, talent and known spells for casters; "tamed by <town>" or "trust N toward #id" for animals.
- **Feed:** Firebolt casts, heals, tamings and refused feeds.
- **God tools:** the spawn cycle (Tab) now includes the dragon.
