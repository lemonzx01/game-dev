## Design
- **Settlements** (`FoundSettlementCommand`, God): name, faction, centre = storage tile, Food/Wood stock (never negative), up to 6 farm plots on the nearest grass. Founding is refused on impassable tiles or within two radii of another town (`TooClose`).
- **Peoples:** `Human` (5) and `Demon` (6) species are omnivores with **identical** numbers and differ only in name and `Faction`, so neither is good or evil by rule. Sapient newcomers (spawned or born) join the nearest settlement of their faction within two radii; members roam inside the town radius.
- **Work** (`ActionKind.Work`, utility 150, below urgent needs): the settlement's need picks the goal, in order: deliver what you carry → harvest a ripe plot (Farmer) → gather wild food if stock < 60 (Gatherer) → cut wood if stock < 30 (Woodcutter). Paths go through the budgeted path queue; an unreachable target gives a work cooldown.
- **Conservation:** wild node / crop → carried (`Gathered` / `Harvested`) → storage (`Deposited`) → eaten at the centre (`EntityAte` with Target = settlement). A carrier who dies loses the load through `CarryLost`. Crops regrow seasonally like wild plants.
- **Display:** `ReadSettlements` / `ReadFarmPlots` read models; Godot draws plots (crop tint), the centre outline in faction colour, zoom-aware names, a towns panel (pop, food, wood, job counts, ripe crops), inspector "lives in … job … carrying …", feed lines for founding and lost loads; God tool E founds towns (Tab = faction), species cycle includes humans and demons. The opening scene founds Ashford (human, west) and Cinderhold (demon, east) with 10 settlers each.
- StateHash/save schema 10 (settlements, plots, members' job/carry/work goal, config).
