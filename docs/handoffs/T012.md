## Design
- **Seasons** come from the integer-tick calendar (T003 `WorldTime`): Spring → Summer → Autumn → Winter, each `DaysPerSeason` days, then a new year.
- **Temperature** (`ClimateConfig`, whole °C) is a pure function of tick and tile: season base (12 / 24 / 10 / −4) + daily swing (±5, warmest at midday) + terrain offset (snow −8, mountain −6, water +2, forest −1). There is no stored state. `ReadTemperature(x, y)` gives a tile's value; `WorldSnapshot.Time.Temperature` carries the open-air value.
- **Seasonal growth:** plant (Food) and Wood regrowth intervals become `RegrowIntervalTicks × 100 / growth%` for the current season (150 / 100 / 60 / 0 %). A dormant season (0 %) or a tile colder than `MinGrowthTemperature` (2 °C) grows nothing and is re-checked after one base interval. Water regrows at its base rate all year. Using intervals instead of fractional amounts needs no carried remainder, so it stays deterministic and saveable.
- **UI:** the Godot HUD shows year, season and day from the snapshot (T007 branch).
- StateHash/save schema 9 (climate config hashed; it is saved with the options).
