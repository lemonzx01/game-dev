## Design
- **Combat (`SimulationWorld.Combat.cs`, `CombatConfig`)**
  - **Valid targets:** every attack is validated with `CanHarm`. Both sides must be alive and different creatures, not townmates, and the target must not be a beast tamed by the attacker's town. The attacker must be adjacent. An invalid attack costs nothing.
  - **Cost:** each attack or spell adds `FatiguePerAttack` fatigue.
  - **Damage:**
    - base: guards are armed (`GuardDamage`), other people fight unarmed (`UnarmedDamage` 150k), animals use their species attack
    - × Combat skill
    - × body condition (50% + 50% × health)
    - × numbers (+15% per ally fighting the same foe within 2 tiles, max 3)
    - × the target's `DamageTakenPercent`
  - **Fighting back:** a non-guard adult person fights a close threat (`ActionKind.Defend`) when cornered, or when brave (≥ 600) with at least 2 townmates nearby. Others still flee.
  - Kills are `Combat` when the killer is defending or is a person, and `Predation` otherwise.
- **Magic (`MagicConfig`)**
  - People who have studied (Scholarship ≥ 1, i.e. one book) can cast.
  - Mana is an elapsed-time anchor: max = 100 + aptitude (0–100, from the seed and id), regenerating 1 point per 20 ticks. People spawn with full mana.
  - **Firebolt** (40 mana, range 4): a defender casts it at a valid foe instead of closing in, every 8 ticks while mana lasts.
  - **Heal** (30 mana, range 3): restores 250k health to the most wounded townmate below 700k, checked on each think.
  - Mana is spent only when a spell is actually cast. Event: `SpellCast` (222).
- **Dragon (species 7, `Data/species.json`):**
  - a real dragon: carnivore, fast, 700k bite, takes 25% damage
  - hunts deer, sheep and wolves
  - `Retaliates`: whoever hurts it becomes its Defend target
  - lifespan 2000 days; it does not breed yet
  - every other species fears it
  - only God can spawn it
- **Taming (`SimulationWorld.Taming.cs`, `TamingConfig`, `ActionKind.Tame`)**
  - **Who:** a safe, fed, adult non-guard townsperson whose town has food above the reserve (40), and only for species with `TameTrust` > 0. Values: deer 60, rabbit 40, wolf 100, dragon 300.
  - **Feeding:** the townsperson walks to the nearest such animal that nobody else is taming and feeds it 1 unit of town food. The animal accepts or refuses with a deterministic roll: 65% − its fear/20 + the feeder's sociability/50, clamped to 5–95.
  - **Trust:** +20 on an accepted feed (the animal also eats), −10 and fear on a refusal. Trust is personal to one feeder. Event: `TameFed` (223).
  - **Tamed:** at TameTrust the animal is tamed by the feeder's town. Event: `AnimalTamed` (224), also stored as a durable milestone in `ReadHistory`.
  - **After taming:**
    - the animal stays within the town radius
    - its people neither fear nor guard against it
    - a tamed hunter spares domestic and tamed animals but still hunts wild game
    - the bond outlives the feeder
- **State, save and reads:** member and animal `Mana/ManaTick/TamedTo/TamerId/Trust`; new species fields; three new configs in the hash. StateHash and save schema 13. New read model `ReadAdventure`.

## Evidence (2026-11-24)
- `dotnet test Tests/Simulation.Tests -c Release -m:1` → 89/89 groups, exit 0. Golden replay re-pinned for schema 13. T009's species count was updated for the dragon. T019 groups:
  1. **Condition and cost:** a guard's first hit at full health = GuardDamage; at half health = 75%. Attacks add fatigue.
  2. **Fight back:** with 9 brave villagers, at least 2 fight the wolf. Only the guard raises GuardAlert. Some hit harder than unarmed alone (ally bonus), the bonus is capped, and the wolf dies in Combat.
  3. **Dragon:**
     - the guard defends against it
     - each hit on the dragon is ≤ a quarter of capped damage
     - the dragon turns on and strikes whoever hurt it, and attacks nobody who didn't
     - villagers flee it
  4. **Magic:**
     - a scholar guard with 50 mana casts exactly one Firebolt (40) from range, and it hits
     - people who never studied never cast
     - Heal picks the badly wounded townmate (+250k), never the lightly scratched one, and spends mana
  5. **Taming:**
     - a deer is fed several times and tamed exactly once
     - every `TameFed` trust value follows +20/−10
     - tamed on reaching 60, as a milestone in ReadHistory
     - town food = before − feeds − eaten + deposited
     - not fed again afterwards and stays near town
     - with food at the reserve, nobody tries to tame
  6. **Tamed wolf:** no GuardAlert against it, its people don't flee it, it never attacks sheep or its people, and it still hunts wild game when hungry.
  7. **Save mid-fight and mid-taming** (with a dragon present): identical continuation.
- Benchmark, 1,000 entities / 3,000 ticks: p50 1.65 ms, p99 2.63 ms. No regression.
