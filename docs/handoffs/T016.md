## Design
- **Coins:** each settlement starts with `StartingCoins` (100) as part of its God founding; afterwards coins only move between settlements through trade.
- **Trade:** a town with ≥ 2 × target of food (then wood) sends one trader (`MaxTradersOut` 1) with up to 8 units to the nearest other town that needs that good and can pay. Trips use whole-map routes from the path queue under their own budget (2 per tick, FIFO kept). At the buyer's centre: buyer storage +units, buyer coins −price × units, seller coins +the same (`TradeSold`, `TradePaid`); unsold units ride home and are deposited; a trader who dies loses the load (`CarryLost`). Humans and demons trade with each other under the same rules.
- **Guards:** one per 8 members (at least one), volunteering on think. A guard sees threats (species its people fear) inside the town radius, raises `GuardAlert`, chases and attacks with `GuardDamage` (350k) instead of fleeing; kills are `DeathCause.Combat` with the guard as killer. Other villagers still flee.
- New: `ActionKind.Defend`, `Job.Guard/Trader`, `WorkGoal.TradeLoad/TradeTravel/ReturnHome`, `TradeConfig`, settlement `Coins/Members/Guards/TradersOut`, member `TradePartner/OnTrip`; StateHash/save schema 11. Godot towns panel shows coins, guards and traders; the feed shows sales, payments and guard alerts.

## Open
- Prices are fixed; no market haggling, caravans, or trade between more than two goods. Guards do not patrol beyond the town or fight people (no war yet, phase 4).
