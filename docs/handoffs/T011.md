## Design
- **Life data per species** (`SpeciesDef` + `Data/species.json`): MaturityTicks, LifespanTicks, GestationTicks, ReproCooldownTicks, LitterSize, LocalDensityCap, BirthHungerCost. LifespanTicks 0 = no ageing and LitterSize 0 = no breeding, so the empty default catalog (older scenarios) is unaffected. Default numbers are provisional balance values.
- **Age and stage:** age = now − BirthTick. Stages Juvenile (< maturity), Adult, Elder (≥ 80 % of lifespan). Lifespan gets ± `LifespanJitterPercent` (10 %) per individual from RNG stream 3. Old-age death is booked once as required work and goes through the shared `Die` path (cause `OldAge`, carcass, durable record). God spawns arrive as adults (BirthTick = now − maturity); `SpawnEntityCommand` takes an optional `Sex`.
- **Reproduction** (`Mate` action, score 400, never under threat): an adult female who is not pregnant and past her cooldown, with hunger and thirst ≤ `ReproMaxNeed`, health ≥ `ReproMinHealth`, sees an adult male of her species and fewer than `LocalDensityCap` same-species animals in perception, approaches him and conceives (`Conceived` event). Birth is required work at the due tick.
- **Caps and resource checks at birth:** the litter is limited by the global `MaxEntities` cap and by what the mother can afford. Each child adds `BirthHungerCost` to her hunger, so offspring cost real food reserves. If she can afford none, `BirthFailed` and no entity. A mother who dies before the due tick loses the pregnancy (stale booking).
- **Children:** new monotonic IDs, BirthTick = birth tick, mother/father recorded (entity, details, death record), born on the mother's tile. Juveniles neither breed nor hunt.
- **Milestones:** births and deaths are durable `ReadHistory` records (one list, ascending EventSequence; birth Actor = mother, Target = child, Amount = father).
- StateHash schema 7.
